Sync lab — the mobile “it keeps trying to sync” loop

A drift correction is a seek, and a seek makes the embedded player refetch: it drops its buffer, shows its loading state, and its status messages keep describing the position it had before the seek. The phone model here does exactly that — a status report arrives lag ms late, and a seek costs load ms of loading (with the old position being re-reported in the meantime). The AFTER column runs the shipped dist/js/player.js; the BEFORE column replays the rule that shipped before it (a 0.75s tolerance, corrected on every status poll). Both see the same 60 seconds of the same phone.

The verdict

Green = the video is playing. Red = the player is loading (its spinner is up). A red bar on the left of a second marks a seek — the correction itself. Note the shape: before, a correction at every poll and the spinner never leaves; after, the join seek, then playback, with a correction only once the budget's cooldown (8s, doubling to 48s) has been spent.

What the budget actually does

  • Tolerances: 2.5s while playing (a playing room keeps its own rhythm), 3.5s in a paused room.
  • A drift needs two observations in the same direction — unless it is ≥12s, which cannot be noise.
  • One correction per cooldown: 8s, doubling to a 48s cap while the player stays wrong. A drift never skips it.
  • Settle windows: 5s after any seek, 3s after a play command — that is the period in which the player is expected to be loading.
  • A frozen clock is loading, not drift: a playing player whose reported position does not advance is backed off, never seeked. Buffering is never a position.
  • The room's own commands (host seek / play / pause) bypass all of it: they arrive through force and land immediately.
  • The room position is projected with a measured clock offset, so a device whose clock is minutes off does not seek for minutes.

Model limits (deliberately pessimistic for the shipped lane): the embed's BUFFERING events are not simulated — in the app they suppress corrections even earlier — and the phone is modelled as a fixed lag plus a fixed load cost, with the room advancing in real time.
