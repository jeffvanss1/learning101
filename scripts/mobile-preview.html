Mobile navigation preview

390×780 phone frame running the SHIPPED markup and stylesheets (dist/index.html's app shell + catalog.css): the left guide rail becomes the bottom bar with Home · Movies · Friends · Series · More, Friends sits in the centre disc, and the rest of the menu is the “More” sheet.

What to look for

  • Five equal slots, no scrolling; Friends is the middle one and the only accent.
  • Tapping More slides the rail in from the left with the remaining entries (Start a room first) and dims the app behind it.
  • A collapsed desktop rail can never shrink the sheet — labels always show.
  • Inside a room the bar disappears (the player keeps the whole screen); the in-room peek brings it back.
  • The bar sits below the content, not over it, and adds the iPhone safe-area padding.

How it is wired

  • One navigator (setupSidenav) feeds the rail, the bar and the sheet, so a destination behaves identically wherever it is tapped.
  • The active slot is painted on both bars from one call; a rail-only destination lights More.
  • body.menu-open reveals the sheet; the X, the backdrop and Escape close it.
