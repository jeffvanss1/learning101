Hero The scrim: art fading into the dark behind the logo

BEFORE is the old 4-stop ramp — solid on the left, then a steep drop (0.6 at 38% → 0.08 at 72%) that reads as a band edge where the dark panel meets the picture. NOW is one long, smoothly eased ramp with nine stops: the artwork fades into the dark under the lockup and keeps its own light on the bright side, with a whisper of shade at the top (for the sticky nav) and a bottom seat that tucks the banner into the page. Same treatment on a phone, where the seat leads because the text stacks.

Now Rows page with a chevron — and the edge fades into the page

A mouse has no horizontal wheel axis and a desktop has no swipe, so a poster row used to be reachable only through its (thin) scrollbar. BEFORE is that row. NOW the row carries a button at each end that still hides cards, and that edge fades into the page under it — --bg, so it is right in both themes and never a black or white wash. One click travels ~86% of the visible width (at least a card), the arrows are positions (they swap sides in RTL), and a row that already fits shows no arrows at all. Hover a row, resize the window, or switch to Light.

Now Trailer embed: YouTube's leftovers, and what covers them

The trailer is a cross-origin YouTube iframe: nothing of ours can reach inside it, and YouTube has no parameter left that removes everything — modestbranding was dropped in August 2023, and the bottom-right watermark is deliberate on their side. So the embed now switches off what it still can (controls=0 kills the control bar and the “Watch on YouTube” button, rel=0 keeps end-screen suggestions on the trailer's own channel, loop=1&playlist=… means the preview never reaches the end screen at all, plus no cards, no captions, no keyboard stealing, no fullscreen), and our own ink covers the rest: a light top shade for the title row and a corner seat where the watermark sits — the same 0.34 → 0 wash the hero uses.
